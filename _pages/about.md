**Ayenew Yihune Demeke** is a PhD student in the Department of Civil, Construction and Environmental Engineering at [North Dakota State University](https://www.ndsu.edu/) (NDSU), Fargo, North Dakota, USA.

Research interests
------
Construction automation, the Internet of Things (IoT), building information modeling (BIM), and reality capture.

Current research focuses on **highway and construction work zone safety** using sensors and data-driven models:

- **Dynamic hazard zone modeling** for proximity detection between workers and construction equipment, using probabilistic (bivariate Gaussian) hazard zones driven by real-time GPS and IMU data ([*Automation in Construction*, 2026](/publication/demeke2026-dynamic-hazard-zone-proximity-detection/); [ISARC 2025](/publication/demeke2025-adaptive-hazard-zone-highway-work-zones/)).
- **Vehicle intrusion detection** in highway work zones using inertial sensors on traffic cones and lightweight deep learning ([*Automation in Construction*, 2025](/publication/younesiheravi2025-vehicle-intrusion-detection-work-zones/)).
- **Sensor technologies for work zone safety**, reviewed systematically for intrusion and proximity hazards ([*Transportation Research Record*, 2025](/publication/demeke2025-work-zone-sensor-technologies-review/)).

Earlier work, carried out at Addis Ababa Science and Technology University (AASTU), Ethiopia, addressed pavement geotechnics: constitutive models for the **resilient modulus of subgrade soils**, evaluated on Long-Term Pavement Performance (LTPP) data ([*Journal of Transportation Engineering, Part B: Pavements*, 2024](/publication/demeke2024-resilient-modulus-models-ltpp/); [*Modeling Earth Systems and Environment*, 2023](/publication/demeke2023-resilient-modulus-four-parameter-model/)).
